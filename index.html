Ninja Control Arts

Movement & Basic Arts

Move your shinobi around the arena using the W, A, S, D keys (or Arrow Keys).

Combat & Jutsu

  • Left Click / J: Basic melee combo strikes (charges 1% Chakra on hit).
  • Right Click / K: Throw Shurikens. Uses ammo (shurikens regenerate over time).
  • Spacebar / L: Chakra Dash (quick invincibility frames to dodge enemy blows).
  • Hold R / O: Charge your Chakra gauge (you remain stationary and vulnerable).
  • Q / I: ULTIMATE JUTSU! Unleashes an screen-clearing strike when your Chakra meter is at 100%.

Scroll Upgrades

Defeated enemies drop scrolls. Collect scrolls during waves to purchase training stats at the training academy between waves.
